Sustainability

At Artevasi, sustainability is not an option, but a commitment we embrace with passion. Our green journey is underpinned by a sustainability strategy for the next four years, based on the three ESG pillars (Environmental, Social and Governance).

Our 2026–2030 strategy

Planet
  • Reduce CO2 emissions and increase renewable energy
  • Use recycled materials and durable products
  • Manage resources and waste responsibly
People
  • Promote diversity and equality
  • Ensure health, safety and continuous training
  • Foster well-being and work-life balance
Governance
  • Act ethically and transparently
  • Create sustainable partnerships
  • Prevent corruption
  • Strengthen our commitment to the client
